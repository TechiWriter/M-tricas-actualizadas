Organic vs. Paid — radar

5 categories, each scored relative to whichever side performed best (100% = strongest run on that axis).

Organic
Paid

Paid stretches furthest on Reach (~16x organic) but pulls back on Returning % and Followers % — a colder, broader audience. Organic stays smaller but consistently closer to the core, warm B2B audience on every other axis.

Side-by-side comparison

Metric Organic Paid
Views / viewers 795 15,000
New viewers 16% 89%
Non-followers reached 39% 95%
Bolivia share of audience 90.8% 78.2%
Male audience 75% 69%
Full video completion — 6.4%

Paid media multiplies raw reach and unlocks new, non-follower audiences (Brazil, USA), but organic reach keeps a tighter demographic match to the core Bolivia B2B segment.

ROI — $5 USD investment

A minimal $5 budget was tested on TikTok Ads to see how far paid reach could stretch a full-spot video, without treating cost-efficiency as the campaign's main goal.

Total spend
$5.00
Cost per 1,000 views
$0.33
Cost per 1,000 viewers
$0.38
Cost per engagement
$0.055
Metric Value
Video views generated 15,000
Unique viewers reached 13,000
Total engagements (likes + comments + shares + saves) 91
Engagement rate on viewers 0.7%
Reach multiple vs. organic (13,000 vs. 795) ~16x

At this spend level, cost-per-view and cost-per-engagement are both extremely low — the $5 test bought a meaningfully larger, if less precisely targeted, audience than the organic run alone. The trade-off is the same one shown in the radar above: paid wins decisively on raw reach, while organic wins on audience precision (local match, returning viewers, existing followers).

DHNews initiative — Issue #1

First issue of Digital Harbor's internal newspaper, available in digital and print format at the Cochabamba and La Paz offices. It included prize-based activities (holographic and magnetic stickers) to encourage active reading, and an open call for comments on which stories stood out most.
